# Audio audit — Below the Abyss

Measured on `main` after PR #46 (2026-11-10). Method: provenance CSV, asset inventory, spectral
analysis of every WAV (FFT per file: peak frequency, spectral centroid, 95 % energy rolloff), and a
read of `scripts/audio/` and `resources/audio/`.

**Limits of this audit.** It is based on measurement and code, not listening. The designer's own
ears are the final judge, and a human sound designer should review it. "Bland" is the designer's
playtest verdict; the numbers below explain why.

## 1. What exists

| Fact | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| 30 audio files, every one generated by repository scripts (`tools/synthesize_audio.py`, `synthesize_feedback_audio.py`, `synthesize_sunlit_music.py`) | `assets/audio-provenance.csv` |
| All are 22 050 Hz, 16-bit; only the music is stereo, the other 29 are mono | provenance + analysis |
| 25 of 30 are short cues (0.55 – 2 s); loops are 2 s (engine, scan, tension) and 8 s (ocean, current) | provenance |
| **One music track** (`sunlit_music.wav`, 32 s loop). Used in every region, fixed gain 0.18, never changes | `soundscape_mixer.gd:157` |
| No voice, no recorded or sampled sound, no Foley, no region-specific ambience | inventory |
| Licence status on every file: "Project license pending; no external source" | provenance |

## 2. What the measurements say

- **18 of the 30 files are single pure tones** (20 within 10 %). Their 95 % energy rolloff sits
  within 5 % of their peak frequency, i.e. one sine with an envelope (examples: `acknowledge`
  989 Hz, `echo` 1049 Hz, `complete` 880 Hz, `ui` 649 Hz, `scan_start` 820 Hz, `dock` 329 Hz,
  `hull` 300 Hz, `mechanical` 180 Hz, `predator_alert` 140 Hz). These read as beeps and hums.
- **Ambience is sub-bass only.** `ocean` and `current` have 95 % of their energy at or below
  73 Hz; `engine` and `tension` at or below 96 Hz. Most laptop and handheld speakers barely
  reproduce this.
- **The music is a low drone.** Centroid 254 Hz, 95 % rolloff 392 Hz, peak 175 Hz. There is no
  melody, harmony, rhythm or texture above 400 Hz.
- **Impact-type cues** (`impact`, `damage`, `geological`, `underwater_blast`) are noise bursts over
  a low tone, the richest sounds in the set, but still single synthesized layers.
- `radio.wav` is the only broadband file (rolloff 9 kHz).

## 3. How the engine plays it

Good scaffold: `SoundscapeMixer` blends five loops by depth, thrust, threat, pressure and scanning,
with ducking; cues have a bus, gain and minimum interval (`resources/audio/shelf_soundscape.tres`);
a Settings panel exposes the mix.

Gaps:
- The bus layout (`default_bus_layout.tres`) has **no effects**: no reverb, low-pass, or
  depth-dependent filtering. Nothing sounds "underwater" or "in a cave".
- **No spatial audio.** Creatures and vents are not positioned in the stereo field.
- Music is one loop with one volume: **no region music, no intensity layers, no stingers**.
- **No Twilight-specific sound at all.** New features reuse generic cues or are silent.

## 4. Game events that have no proper sound

| Event (shipped) | Current audio | Needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rift Stalker warning, pursuit, flee, stun | reuses `predator_alert` / `predator_pursue` (140 Hz / 89 Hz tones) | distinct creature voice, approach sound, escape stinger |
| Thermal vent warning and eruption | none or generic | rumble build-up, vent roar, distance falloff |
| Torpedo launch, flight, hit, rock gate opening | `impact` / `underwater_blast` only | launch thud, propeller wake, detonation, rock-crack and debris |
| Tunnel squeeze scrape / collision | `impact` | hull scrape, metal stress, tight-space reverb |
| Depth hull stress and buckling | `creak` | escalating groans, rivet pops, final collapse |
| Hull failure and respawn | none | failure sting, respawn cue |
| Net animal / sentinel | `biological` (548 Hz tone) | living, readable vocalisation, release and capture sounds |
| Mara's logs and Wick's radio lines | text only, `radio.wav` blip | recorded voice (even degraded) |
| Arrival at 350 m, tunnel discovery, deep basin | none | place-defining ambience and a music change |

## 5. Recommendations, in order of impact

1. **Replace the music.** One composed, region-specific score with layers (calm / uneasy / hunted),
   stereo, 44.1 or 48 kHz, ≥ 90 s loops, delivered as stems. Highest atmosphere per cost.
2. **Sound design for the shipped threat and hazard events** in section 4, recorded or layered from
   real sources (not single sine tones). Priority: stalker, vents, torpedo and rock gate, depth stress.
3. **Voice for Mara and Wick**, with radio processing. Even a few lines change how the story lands.
4. **Engine work** (a Codex issue, after assets exist): per-region ambience beds, bus effects
   (reverb by space, low-pass by depth), spatial `AudioStreamPlayer2D` for creatures and vents,
   music layer mixing driven by the existing threat value and new region state.
5. **Keep the code-generated set** as fallback and for tests until replaced; do not delete it.

## 6. Asset acceptance spec (for any human or licensed source)

- Format: 44.1 or 48 kHz, 16- or 24-bit WAV masters; ship music as Ogg Vorbis, short cues as WAV.
- Stereo for music and ambience beds; mono for positional emitters.
- Loops: sample-accurate, no click, ≥ 60 s music, ≥ 30 s ambience beds.
- Provenance row per file in `assets/audio-provenance.csv`: author, licence and its text, source
  URL or contract reference, and rights for Steam distribution and commercial use.
- **No asset of unclear licence, no AI-generated audio claimed as human, no unlicensed samples**
  (AGENTS.md: do not invent licensed assets or claim rights to generated or third-party media).
- Peak ≤ −1 dBFS; loudness consistent per category; keep the existing accessibility rule that
  every critical audio cue has a visual or text equivalent.
